ABOUT ME

WHO I AM?

Hi! I am Huy Hung Nguyen, a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Software Engineering at Auburn University, where I conduct research with Prof. Anh Totti Nguyen. I expect to graduate in Spring 2027.

My research focuses on Vision-Language Models (VLMs), multimodal reasoning, interpretable AI, and computer vision. I am especially interested in understanding how multimodal models reason about visual information, when they make mistakes, and how their reasoning can be made more transparent and useful.

Before starting my Ph.D., I earned my M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Seoul National University. I also worked in industry as an AI R&D team leader and manager, developing and deploying computer vision systems for eKYC, face recognition, anomaly detection, and intelligent video analytics. This industry experience complements my current research in multimodal AI.

I am seeking full-time AI/ML Research Engineer, Machine Learning Engineer, and research-oriented opportunities, with an expected graduation in Spring 2027. I'm also open to research collaborations and technical discussions. Feel free to reach out if you're interested in my research or would like to discuss potential projects.

Research: My primary interests include:

1. Vision-Language Models and Visual Reasoning. Understanding how multimodal models interpret images, reason about physical and visual information, and communicate explanations with users. My recent work includes SketchVLM.

2. Interpretable and Interactive AI. Developing approaches for understanding, debugging, and improving the behavior of machine learning models, particularly those that combine language and vision.

3. Computer Vision and Applied AI. Visual correspondence, object-level change detection, recognition, tracking, and AI systems that solve practical problems.
