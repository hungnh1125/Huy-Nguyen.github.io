01 / ABOUT

About me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Software Engineering at Auburn University, working with Prof. Anh Totti Nguyen and Prof. Jiaqi Wang. My work focuses on understanding and improving vision-language models, with particular interests in interpretable and interactive AI.

Before joining Auburn, I earned my M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Seoul National University and worked in AI R&D roles in industry. I have led teams developing computer vision applications for identity verification, video analytics, and production systems.

I'm interested in connecting academic advances in multimodal AI with practical, reliable machine learning applications.
