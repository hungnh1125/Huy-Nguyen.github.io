About Me

Who I Am?

Hi! I am Hung Huy Nguyen, a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Software Engineering at Auburn University, where I work with Prof. Anh Totti Nguyen and Prof. Jiaqi Wang.

My research centers on vision-language models, multimodal reasoning, and interpretable AI. I am interested in understanding how AI systems reason about visual information, how their behavior can be explained and improved, and how they can better support human users.

Before starting my Ph.D., I earned an M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Seoul National University. I also worked in industry, leading teams that developed computer vision and applied machine learning systems for identity verification, intelligent video analytics, and banking applications.

I am always open to research collaborations, discussions, and opportunities in AI/ML research and engineering. Feel free to reach out.

Research interests:
  • Vision-Language Models & Multimodal AI: Visual reasoning, model understanding, and image-grounded communication.
  • Interpretable & Interactive AI: Understanding model behavior and enabling human intervention.
  • Computer Vision: Visual correspondence, change detection, recognition, and real-world systems.
